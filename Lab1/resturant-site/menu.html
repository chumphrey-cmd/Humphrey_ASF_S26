Our Philosophy

Our Menu Vision

Welcome to our cafe and bakery, where we believe that a beautiful plate is better than an ugly one.

Our culinary philosophy is straightforward: simple, handcrafted flavors are better than complex, over-engineered dishes.

We Value Ingredient Quality

When it comes to what goes into our food, explicit is better than implicit. We proudly and openly source our heritage grains from local mills and our produce from local greenhouses.

Breakfast

Berry & Banana French Toast

Maple syrup, powdered sugar, and a whole lot of love. Our signature french toast is the perfect way to start your morning. Served with fresh, seasonal berries and perfectly ripe bananas.

Breakfast

Eggs Benedict

Two perfectly poached eggs on a toasted English muffin, layered with thick-cut smoked ham and topped with our house-made hollandaise sauce. Served with a side of roasted breakfast potatoes.

Lunch

Bacon & Egg Croissant

A buttery, flaky croissant baked fresh this morning, stuffed with thick-cut crispy bacon, an over-easy egg, and a slice of sharp cheddar cheese. Comes with a side of mixed fruit.

Lunch

Steak Tenderloin with Roasted Cabbage

A perfectly seared, tender cut of steak served alongside char-roasted cabbage. Finished with a light herb butter and a sprinkle of flaky sea salt for a clean, savory bite.

Breakfast & Lunch

Iced Coffee

Our signature house blend, cold-brewed for 18 hours to ensure a smooth, low-acidity finish. Served over ice with your choice of milk and optional house-made simple syrup.

Breakfast & Lunch

Iced Matcha

Premium ceremonial grade matcha, delicately whisked and poured over ice. Blended with creamy oat milk and lightly sweetened with organic agave.
